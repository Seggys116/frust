Forcing the vectorizer on that locked 1.26 loop does not produce the kernel either. A throwaway copy, registry sha256 `a46dae12f2020b68d20c965ae43f9710e3e792bf77f3ca4611582f8d8d0c9541` before and after, was compiled `-O3 -fno-inline` so `deflate_find_min_cost_path` stayed a symbol. Clang 21, Clang 23.1.5, and GCC 15.2 `-mavx2` each left the symbol scalar: the inner loop is a `cmpl` / `jae` and a stride-8 add, and it has no `ymm` operand. The pragma `vectorize(enable) vectorize_width(8)` reports `loop not vectorized (Force=true, Vector Width=8, Interleave Count=1)`. `-force-vector-width=8` puts 65 `vpminud` instructions into other loops of the Clang 21 object and none into this function. Sinking the `item` store into two scalars does not change that. LLVM's remark on the loop shape is `value that could not be identified as reduction is used outside the loop`. The length kept with the winning cost is an argmin, and LLVM 23.1.5-rust-1.100.0-stable has no flag that turns an argmin into a reduction. A pure minimum of the same adds does vectorize to `vpminud` at width 8, including a stride-8 load. That is the form the unreleased rewrite already uses, and stock Clang vectorizes it, so the stock control would gain the same code. No level-12 timing was run, because the locked function's instructions did not change. `frust-gates` was not run. `optimize-linux-v2.sh` and `opt-dist` were still alive (load 6.69). Detail is in `target/compiler-rewrite/logs/vecmin-locked.md`.
